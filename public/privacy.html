Atlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Atlas is operated by Hero Vehicle Care, Sanford, Florida, USA ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Atlas handles and why.

Information we handle

  • Your account: name, email, password (stored securely by our authentication provider), and business details such as business name, services and prices.
  • Your customers' information that you enter or import: names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, vehicles, notes, quotes, jobs and message history. If you connect a lead source such as Facebook lead ads, the contact details a person submits there are added to your customer list.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address and device/browser information, used to keep the Service secure and working.

How we use it

To run Atlas for you: show your data to you, send quotes and messages you choose to send, keep the Service secure, fix problems, and answer support requests. We do not sell personal information and do not use your customers' information for our own marketing.

Who your customers' data belongs to

For the customer information you put into Atlas, you decide what to collect and how to use it; we act as your service provider and handle it only on your instructions. If one of your customers asks you to access or delete their information, you can do it in Atlas or ask us to help.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run Atlas: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (web hosting), and Quo/OpenPhone (delivering text messages you send). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect against fraud or abuse. Your data is not visible to other Atlas businesses.

Text messages

When you send a quote by text, the customer's phone number and the message are passed to our messaging provider for delivery. Messages include "Reply STOP to opt out." You are responsible for having permission to message your customers.

Security and retention

We use reputable providers and access controls so each business can only see its own data. No system is perfectly secure. We keep your data while your account is active, and delete it when you ask us to, except where we must keep something for legal reasons.

Your choices

You can edit or delete records in Atlas, and you can ask us to export or delete your account and data. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your information. Contact us and we will respond promptly.

Children

Atlas is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
